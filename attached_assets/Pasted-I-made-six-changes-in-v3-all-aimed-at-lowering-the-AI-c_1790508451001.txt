I made six changes in v3, all aimed at lowering the AI cost.

1. Free filter before the AI

Before: anything with a price or reference-like text went to the AI, including room ads, because postcodes like "E15" looked like references.
Now: a message goes to the AI only if it mentions a watch word (brand, model, WTS/WTB, full set, dial, bezel…) or a real reference shape (126710BLNR, 116500, 5711/1A, WSSA0018).
Messages about rooms, rent, licences, driving, jobs, loans or delivery accounts are skipped for free, unless they also mention a watch.

2. 8 messages per AI call

Before: one AI call per message, and each call carried about 1,650 tokens of instructions.
Now: up to 8 messages go in one call, so the instructions are paid once for all 8. If the answer doesn't fit, the batch is split in half automatically.

3. Shorter instructions and compact answers

Before: instructions were about 1,650 tokens, and each watch came back with every field written out, including empty ones (about 140 tokens).
Now: instructions are about 1,100 tokens.
The AI answers with short codes (e.g. cond: N, set: F) and skips empty fields, which is about 60% smaller.
Instead of copying the message line back, it gives the line number and the tool looks up the text itself.

4. Auto-pause for groups with no watches

If a group has 40 messages checked by the AI and none contain a watch, the AI stops for that group.
Its messages are still saved, marked "paused". Admin shows "paused" with a Resume button, and there's a Pause button for any group.

5. Cost meter

Every AI call records its token usage.
Admin now shows AI cost (24h), AI cost (7 days) and Skipped: AI paused, plus an AI (7 days) cost column per group.

6. Safe upgrade

Database auto-update: when a new version adds columns, the app adds them itself on start. Your existing data isn't touched.
docker-compose.apache.yml: a version for your server where Apache handles the domain. It has no Caddy and keeps the app on 127.0.0.1:18080.
try_parse shows cost: the test command now also prints the tokens used and the cost of the call.

New settings in .env (optional, the defaults are fine):

AI_BATCH_SIZE=8        # messages per AI call
AUTO_PAUSE_AFTER=40    # 0 = never auto-pause

Not changed: what gets extracted (the same fields), the model (still Haiku, the cheapest), and every other page.
